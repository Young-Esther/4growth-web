On August 24, 2026, 4growth filed a patent application for a zone-based supplemental lighting control method and system. It covers DLight's control method, which measures natural light and uses LEDs to supply only what is missing from the target. The work is an outcome of the 2026 Youth Green Startup Spring Camp program of Korea's Ministry of Environment and KEITI.
